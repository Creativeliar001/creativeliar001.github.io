About

A product manager with an operator's discipline.

I'm a product manager at Aeonian Technology, where I own strategy, roadmaps, and delivery for three SaaS platforms and report directly to the CEO. I'm also completing an MS in Technology Management at North Carolina A&T State University, with a 4.0 GPA.

Over the past ten years I've worked across product, operations, and programs. At The CANs, I ran platform operations for UNSUB and grew it from 17 to 2,000 users across 122 partner organizations in two months. As a consultant, I redesigned client workflows and helped leaders make better decisions with data.

I let evidence guide the work. As a Lean Six Sigma Green Belt, I use DMAIC, process capability analysis, regression, and forecasting to find what's holding a process back and to measure whether a change worked. I also design and build tools of my own.
