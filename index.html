About

A builder who learned to explain things first.

I'm a graduate student in Technology Management at North Carolina A&T State University, focused on Six Sigma and quality systems. My day-to-day tools are process data, regression and forecasting models, and the people who run the machines.

I came to engineering through communication. I spent six years after my undergraduate degree leading communication and stakeholder engagement for a nonprofit in Abuja that built digital platforms against gender-based violence, police brutality, and poverty. A stint as a product manager at a fintech company showed me how much I liked building the product itself.

Since then I've earned the ACE CNC certificate and gone on to train others in CNC machining and metrology. I also build AI-powered web apps: a job search assistant, a volunteer coordination platform, and a lab operations system for the Applied Engineering Department at NC A&T State University.

In development

AET Lab Operations Platform

For NC A&T Applied Engineering Technology

A web platform for running the department's CNC, automotive, and fabrication labs. Students scan a machine's QR code to book time, check out tools, and report problems. Anyone without current safety training is blocked.

  • QR booking and checkout for every machine and tool
  • Problem reports with photo or video from the floor
  • Safety-training gate enforced at booking
  • AI-triaged repair tickets for the lab manager
  • Preventive maintenance schedules and digital 5S audits
  • Chair dashboard with machine uptime, open issues, and 5S scores
Built

AI Job Search Assistant

Matching · Drafting · Tracking

Finds well-matched roles every day and sends them at the time you choose, ranked by fit with a short summary of each. For the jobs you pick, it drafts a tailored, ATS-ready CV, cover letter, and application answers using only facts you've confirmed.

  • Daily ranked matches delivered on your schedule
  • Tailored documents built only from confirmed facts
  • Submits on approval, or hands you everything to apply yourself
  • Saved job descriptions for every application
  • Follow-ups and interview prep
  • Weekly insights on what's earning callbacks
In development

Volunteer Coordination Platform

For nonprofits, NGOs & churches

Gets the right people to show up and takes the paperwork off coordinators' plates. Volunteers sign up, confirm, swap, and check in by text, with no account or app.

  • Text-based sign-up, confirmation, and shift swaps
  • Automatic reminders and waitlist backfill
  • QR check-in that logs volunteer hours
  • Live event view for coordinators
  • One-click verification letters for volunteer hours
  • Funder-ready reports on impact and in-kind match
