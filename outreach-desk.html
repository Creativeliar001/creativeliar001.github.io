Aeon Outreach Desk

An AI outreach agent I designed and deployed at Aeonian Technology. It replaced hours of manual prospecting for Aeon School, a live bus tracking platform, with a pipeline that runs every day on its own.

01

The problem

While managing Aeon School, I saw that manual client prospecting had become the bottleneck slowing growth.

Finding the right organizations, researching each one, and writing a message that fit them took hours of work every time. The product was ready to sell, but the top of the sales funnel depended on how much time someone could spend on research and writing that week.

02

Defining the customer

Before automating anything, I defined who Aeon School is for. The target profile covers the organizations that move children and young people every day:

School bus operators

The core use case for live bus tracking.

Shuttle & charter services

Operators running scheduled and booked trips.

Day camps & youth programs

Organizations that transport the young people in their care.

03

How it works

  1. Find. Every day, the agent searches the web for organizations in the US and Canada that match the target profile, and keeps only verified prospects.
  2. Tailor. For each prospect, it writes a personalized email based on the people that organization serves and the Aeon School features most relevant to its use case.
  3. Send. Emails go out through the company inbox, using a Power Automate and OneDrive integration, so every message comes from a legitimate business address rather than a generic sender.
04

Product decisions

  • Relevance over volume. Each email speaks to what that organization does, instead of a single template sent to everyone.
  • Credibility of the sender. Routing through the company inbox protects the brand and makes the outreach look like what it is: a real company reaching out.
  • No ongoing effort. The system had to run without anyone babysitting it, or it would become another task competing for the same hours.
05

Results

What used to take hours of manual research and writing now happens automatically every day.

Hundredsof potential clients reached per week
Dailyruns, with no human input
Noneongoing effort needed to maintain it

The pipeline it generates is consistent and scalable, and it keeps running whether or not anyone has time for prospecting that week.
