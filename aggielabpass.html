AggieLabPass

A production lab-operations app for NC A&T's Applied Engineering Technology labs. It started with one interview with the lab director, and I took it from discovery to a deployed product.

01

Discovery

Before designing anything, I interviewed the director of the Applied Engineering Technology labs to understand how the labs actually run day to day.

The labs serve classes, research, and student groups, with shared tools, consumables, and CNC, automotive, and fabrication equipment. The interview surfaced four recurring problems. None of them was a technology problem on its own. Each one came from the lab having no shared, up-to-date record of what was happening.

What the lab director describedWhat it cost the lab
Untracked toolsTools left the tool room with no record of who had them.
Surprise stockoutsConsumables ran out before anyone knew to reorder.
Unreported breakagesBroken tools went unreported until the shelf was empty.
Noise clashing with classesLoud machine work collided with teaching in the same space.
02

From problems to features

Every feature traces back to one of the four problems. I used a Lean lens throughout: make the right action the easy one, and make problems visible as soon as they happen.

ProblemWhat I built
Untracked toolsQR-code tool checkout, with overdue reminders that escalate to the Lab Manager.
Surprise stockoutsLive stock counts and an automatic reorder list.
Unreported breakagesBroken-tool tracking, so damage is logged when it happens instead of discovered later.
Noise clashing with classesMachine booking with noise-conflict warnings.
03

Designing for the people who use it

A lab tool only works if students can use it in the middle of lab work, in seconds. That shaped the design:

  • QR codes everywhere. Students scan to check out a tool or check in to a session. Auto-generated QR label sheets make it easy for staff to tag new tools and shelves.
  • Training-gated booking. Students can only book machines they have been cleared to use. Safety is enforced by the system instead of by memory, a mistake-proofing approach from Lean.
  • Session sign-up with QR check-in, so lab sessions have an accurate attendance record.
  • Role-based access for 4 user roles, so each person sees what they need and nothing they shouldn't.
  • Sign-in with an NC A&T email code. No passwords to create or forget.
04

Built to improve the lab over time

The app does more than replace a clipboard. It gives the lab data it never had.

Overdue checkouts, breakage records, stock levels, and booking conflicts are now captured as they happen. That gives the Lab Manager and faculty a factual basis for reordering, maintenance, and scheduling decisions, the same Plan-Do-Check-Act loop I apply as a Lean Six Sigma Green Belt.

Discovery to production, as the only person on the product.

I owned every stage: the interview, the problem definition, the feature set, the build, and the deployment.
