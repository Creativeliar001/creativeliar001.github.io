About

Development work, run like a product.

Before moving to the United States, I spent years with nonprofit organizations in Nigeria on initiatives addressing gender-based violence, police accountability, and COVID-19 relief for vulnerable communities. At The CANs I led platform operations and stakeholder engagement across 122 partner organizations.

That work taught me that impact depends on adoption. A platform only matters when the people it serves use it, and a program only works when every partner delivers. So I bring product and quality discipline to development work: clear requirements from field feedback, structured onboarding, and reporting that funders can trust.

In North Carolina I've continued that work through advocacy media, education resources, and service to the international student community.
